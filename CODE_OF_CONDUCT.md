# Code of Conduct

ReShip contributors are expected to keep technical collaboration professional, specific, and focused on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Review code and ideas rather than attacking contributors. Give actionable technical criticism, disclose conflicts of interest, and respect security-reporting boundaries.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or moderate contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

For conduct concerns involving a maintainer, use a private contact channel rather than a public issue when feasible.
